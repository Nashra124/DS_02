4. UPLIFT MODEL - T-learner built with LightGBM (uplift_model.py)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT: The retention offer and the customer response are SIMULATED,
because the Telco dataset has no real offer data. These results show that
the method can find customers who respond to an offer. They do not prove
that a real offer would perform this well.

CausalML could not be installed on Windows (it needs Microsoft C++ Build
Tools), so the T-learner (the same method CausalML implements) was built
directly with two LightGBM models.

Evaluation on held-out customers (30% test split):

  group        treated_retention  control_retention  real_uplift
  Top 20%            0.572              0.365           0.207
  20-40%             0.647              0.677          -0.030
  40-60%             0.904              0.893           0.011
  60-80%             0.964              0.938           0.027
  Bottom 20%         0.854              0.829           0.025

Reading: the offer raises retention by about 21 percentage points in the
Top 20% group, and has almost no effect in the other groups. The -0.030
value is within noise for groups of this size.


5. CLV AND SEGMENTATION (final_table.py)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

6. ROI SIMULATOR (Streamlit app.py)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings: 20% of customers targeted, 10% discount, lasting 6 months.
Offer response is simulated (see section 4).

  Targeting strategy        Net benefit
  Highest uplift            $1,140,296  (ROI 1,681%)
  Highest churn risk        $907,734  (ROI 1,357%)
  Random                    $295,598  (ROI539%)
The Highest uplift strategy targets customers who respond to the offer,
not only customers likely to leave.


7. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Offer and response are simulated (no real treatment data in Telco).
- No complaints or usage-trend columns exist in this dataset, so features
  are tenure, contract, services and charges, plus engineered
  num_services and charge_vs_average.
- The ROI numbers depend on assumptions: the simulated response, a linear
  scaling of the uplift with discount size, and CLV capped at 72 months.
- The classifier was evaluated on one train/test split.
